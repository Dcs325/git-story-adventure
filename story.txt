Adrenaline surged through Chloe's veins, shattering her paralysis as the footsteps quickened. She bolted toward the exit, her sneakers slapping loudly against the cracked concrete floor. Behind her, a heavy metal pipe crashed onto the ground right where she had been standing. She threw her weight against the rusted emergency door, bursting out into the blinding, beautiful safety of the afternoon sun.
The footsteps above stopped directly overhead, casting a massive shadow down upon her. Chloe froze, her heart hammering against her ribs as she looked up into the darkness. A metallic click echoed through the empty space, followed by a voice she knew all too well. "I told you not to look for me, Chloe," her brother whispered from the catwalk. Before she could scream, the station lights went completely black.
